MRPL39 is an essential mitochondrial ribosomal large-subunit protein required for mitochondrial translation, mitoribosome stability, and oxidative phosphorylation (OXPHOS) integrity. Biallelic pathogenic variants cause mitoribosomal large-subunit instability, impaired mitochondrial protein synthesis, OXPHOS defects, and multisystem mitochondrial disease including Leigh syndrome spectrum.

The supplied text provides no direct evidence that MRPL39 is dysregulated in Alzheimer's disease, contributes causally to amyloid-β or tau pathology, synaptic loss, neuroinflammation, blood-brain barrier dysfunction, or cognitive decline, nor that it has been therapeutically modulated in any AD model.
