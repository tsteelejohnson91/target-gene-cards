How the cards and scores were made
  • For each gene, PubMed was searched by gene symbol and up to the 500 most recent abstracts were retrieved (in batches of 100). ChatGPT-5.5 summarized them in batches of 15 abstracts and then condensed those batch summaries into one overview. Summarizing existing text, rather than generating it de novo, reduces hallucination.
  • Claude Sonnet 4.6 converted each summary into an HTML card with a mechanism figure; several candidates were generated per gene and the one with the fewest figure-layout problems was kept.
  • Swiss tournament score (0–10): gene summaries were compared head-to-head in a Swiss-system tournament (10 rounds; win = 1, draw = 0.5, loss = 0). The score is the mean over 5 independent tournament runs; higher means the summary was judged the stronger Alzheimer's target case more often.
