Key Insights

Core ECM Function

CHADL's primary established role is in skeletal and cartilage tissues — binding collagen, inhibiting collagen fibrillogenesis in vitro, and maintaining pericellular matrix homeostasis in adult chondrocytes and fetal cartilage.

Chondrocyte Regulation

CHADL knockdown in chondrogenic cells increased markers of chondrocyte differentiation and matrix deposition, indicating that CHADL acts as a negative regulator of chondrocyte maturation and ECM accumulation.

Strong OA Genetic Link

A rare frameshift variant in CHADL is associated with markedly increased risk of total hip replacement and earlier replacement age. Transcriptomic studies further classify CHADL as a possible osteoarthritis treatment target based on differential expression in OA-relevant tissues.

Mouse Model Phenotypes

Chadl loss-of-function mouse models display altered intervertebral disc structure, vertebral bone properties, collagen fiber organization, matrix staining, mineral density, and disc-height phenotypes — all reinforcing a skeletal ECM homeostasis role.
