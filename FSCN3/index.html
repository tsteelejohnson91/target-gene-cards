FSCN3 is a member of the fascin family of actin-binding and actin-bundling proteins. Unlike its paralogs, FSCN3 is characterized by highly testis-specific expression, with the most prominent localization in elongating spermatids. Its best-characterized biological roles concern spermatogenesis, spermatid head elongation, and actin cytoskeletal remodeling in germ cells.

The provided evidence base does not support FSCN3 as an Alzheimer's disease mechanism gene or therapeutic target.
