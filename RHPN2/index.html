Key Insights

Hypertension Epigenomics

A longitudinal EWAS identified RHPN2 among genes with blood-pressure–associated DNA methylation trajectories over ~7 years in leukocytes. Because hypertension is a vascular risk factor for cognitive decline and AD-related dementia, RHPN2 may warrant study in vascular-aging cohorts — but no brain tissue or dementia outcomes were assessed.

RhoA / ROCK Signaling Hub

RHPN2 directly binds RhoA in zebrafish, reduces ROCK2b expression upon knockout, and shows heightened RhoA activation linked to the rs10411210 risk allele. Structural defects are rescued by RhoA activation or rock2b mRNA injection, establishing a clear mechanistic axis. RhoA/ROCK biology influences cytoskeletal dynamics, neurite structure, synapses, vascular tone, and inflammatory responses — all AD-adjacent processes.
