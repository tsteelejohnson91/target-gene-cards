PARP8 belongs to the PARP enzyme family and has been detected with in vivo cysteine ADP-ribosylation under physiological conditions. The current relevance of PARP8 to Alzheimer's disease is indirect: PARP8 appears repeatedly in contexts involving ADP-ribosylation, oxidative stress, apoptosis, immune activation, ferroptosis, DNA damage and repair, metabolic reprogramming, and tissue injury — pathways that broadly overlap with recognised AD mechanisms. This makes PARP8 a plausible exploratory biomarker or mechanistic candidate, but not a validated AD therapeutic target.

Key Insights

No Direct AD Evidence

No study has examined PARP8 in AD brain tissue, cerebrospinal fluid, blood, or AD-relevant cell types. PARP8 manipulation has never been shown to affect amyloid, tau, neurodegeneration, synaptic loss, neuroinflammation, or cognition.

PARP Family & ADP-Ribosylation

PARP8 performs in vivo cysteine ADP-ribosylation under physiological conditions. Because AD involves oxidative DNA damage, impaired repair, and dysregulated stress signalling, PARP8 could influence neuronal or glial stress responses — but this remains entirely untested in AD.

Oxidative Stress & Apoptosis Link

In acute retinal degeneration induced by hydrogen peroxide, Parp8 expression increased alongside pro-apoptotic markers Casp3, Casp9, and Bax, while Bcl2 decreased. This links PARP8 to oxidative-stress-associated degeneration — relevant to, but not specific for, AD.

Immune & Inflammatory Biomarker

PARP8 is identified as an immune/inflammatory biomarker in ulcerative colitis, renal fibrosis, tuberculosis, and cancer. It correlates with neutrophils, M1 macrophages, and activated CD4⁺ T cells — potentially relevant if similar programs occur in AD microglia or infiltrating immune populations.

Ferroptosis & Metabolic Connections

PARP8 appears in ferroptosis-associated gene signatures in inflammatory disease and infection datasets. Iron accumulation, lipid peroxidation, and antioxidant failure — hallmarks of ferroptosis — are implicated in AD, warranting investigation of PARP8 in AD ferroptosis models.

Mouse Model Genomic Caveat

Parp8 is among genes affected by polymorphic L1 retrotransposon insertions in laboratory mouse strains. Expression or gene structure may differ across AD model backgrounds, potentially influencing disease phenotypes and requiring explicit verification in any given strain.
