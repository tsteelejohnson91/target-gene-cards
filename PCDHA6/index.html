As a therapeutic target for Alzheimer's disease, PCDHA6 remains entirely speculative. It is most plausibly regarded as a candidate marker or modulator of synaptic integrity and neuronal adhesion, warranting exploratory investigation rather than near-term drug development.

Hippocampal Plasticity Signal

In a seasonal brain-size plasticity study (Eurasian common shrew), PCDHA6 was identified among genes linked to neurogenesis and hippocampal gene-expression changes — suggesting relevance to plasticity mechanisms, though not to AD or neurodegeneration.

Neurite-Growth Association (DISC1 Model)

PCDHA6 expression was decreased in DISC1-mutant iPSC-derived excitatory neurons exhibiting impaired neurite outgrowth. However, functional rescue implicated UNC5D, not PCDHA6 — making it a transcriptional co-signature rather than a proven causal regulator.

Fyn & Reelin Pathway Bridges

Peripheral Regulatory & Developmental Clues

A monocyte miRNA-SNP study found trans-regulation of PCDHA6 by hsa-miR-1279 rs1463335. A missense variant appeared in a short-root anomaly exome study. Neither finding establishes a role in AD neuroinflammation or brain immune mechanisms.
