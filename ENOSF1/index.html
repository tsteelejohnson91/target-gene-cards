Key Insights

🚫 No Direct Alzheimer's Evidence

The supplied studies report no altered ENOSF1 expression, genetic association, protein function, or therapeutic modulation in Alzheimer's patients, Alzheimer's brain tissue, or any Alzheimer's model system.

🔗 TYMS-Linked Antisense Regulator

ENOSF1/rTS is a naturally occurring antisense regulator or overlapping transcript system that can influence TYMS biology, potentially including post-transcriptional silencing of thymidylate synthase expression.

🧬 Nucleotide & Genome Maintenance

In dyskeratosis congenita, TYMS variants combined with ENOSF1 haplotypes caused severe TYMS deficiency, altered dNTP pools, genotoxic stress, defective transcription, and abnormal telomere maintenance — broadly aging-relevant mechanisms.

⚡ Mitochondrial Relevance

ENOSF1/rTS isoforms (rTSβ, rTSγ) were detected in human mitochondria. Because mitochondrial dysfunction is central to Alzheimer's biology, this represents a plausible but unproven mechanistic bridge.

💊 Pharmacogenomics — Best-Supported Role

The variant rs2612091/c.742-227G>A is robustly associated with capecitabine/5-fluorouracil toxicity, hand-foot syndrome, dose reduction, and treatment outcomes in gastrointestinal cancers. This firmly positions ENOSF1 as a cancer pharmacogenomic biomarker.

🌐 Indirect Brain & Metabolic Signals

ENOSF1 appeared in computational brain expression networks (lipid, DNA repair, circadian, inflammatory pathways), in vascular/atherosclerosis methylation profiles, and as a modifier of metformin glycemic response — all indirect and not Alzheimer's-specific.
