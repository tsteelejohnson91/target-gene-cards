COL4A4's established function is structural: it contributes to the collagen IV α3α4α5 network, most critically in the glomerular basement membrane. Its primary biological role is maintaining filtration barrier integrity, not neurological signaling.

Disease Association — Alport Spectrum

Genetic Heterogeneity & Clinical Variability

COL4A4 disease is genetically heterogeneous. Heterozygous variants may cause isolated hematuria or progressive kidney disease; biallelic variants cause autosomal recessive Alport syndrome; digenic interactions with COL4A3 or COL4A5 can modify severity.
